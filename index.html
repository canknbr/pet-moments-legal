Pet Moments

Medication & Vaccine Tracker

Every dose on time and every vaccine on record for your dog or cat, with the photos you never want to lose, in one place.

Pet Moments tracks medications and dose times, reminds you before a vaccine is due and keeps vet visits and weight in order. Start free without an account, share care with family when you need to, and hand your vet a clean record in seconds.

MEDICATIONS AND DOSES

  • Add each medication with its dose times and get a reminder for every dose
  • Mark a dose as given with one tap and see today's schedule at a glance
  • Follow adherence and dose streaks, with a heads-up when a refill is due
  • Keep prescription photos next to the medication they belong to

VACCINES, VET VISITS AND WEIGHT

  • Record vaccinations and see which are current, due soon or overdue
  • Get a reminder when a vaccination is coming up
  • Log vet visits, conditions and the microchip number
  • Follow weight on a chart that shows the trend
  • Create a vet packet PDF with the records your vet asks for

A DIARY OF YOUR LIFE TOGETHER

  • Save photos with a note, mark favorites and keep them in albums
  • On This Day brings back a memory from the same day in earlier years
  • Share a moment as a story card
  • If a pet passes away, their records and photos stay with you

DAILY CARE

  • Log meals, water, walks, grooming and potty breaks with one tap
  • See what is left for today and build care streaks
  • Get a weekly report for each pet

SHARED CARE

  • Invite family, a sitter or a dog walker
  • Assign tasks and see who did what
  • Choose who can edit and who can only view

PRIVACY

  • No ads, and your data is never sold
  • Start without an account; sign in to sync across devices
  • Export your data whenever you like

Free includes 1 pet, 1 active medication with 1 dose time, and 5 photos, plus daily care, basic health records and data export. Premium unlocks unlimited pets, medications, dose times and photos, shared care, prescription photos and the vet packet.

Pet Moments organizes your pet's care records. For medical questions, always talk to your vet.
