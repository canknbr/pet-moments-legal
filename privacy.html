Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026

1. Who we are

Pet Moments ("the app") is provided by Can Kanbur ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who processes it for us, and the choices you have. It applies to the Pet Moments app for iPhone.

Contact: cankanbur1@gmail.com

2. What we collect

Information you give us:

  • Pet profiles, care and activity logs, health records (medications, vaccinations, vet visits, weights, documents) and the photos you add
  • If you create an account: your email address, a display name, and your password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider). If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple shares your name and an email address, which may be a private relay address
  • People you invite to share care of a pet: their email address, and the role you give them
  • Support messages you send us

Information collected automatically:

  • A push notification token for this device, if you allow notifications
  • Your device's time zone and the app language, so reminders arrive at the right local time and in your language
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data when the app fails (device model, OS version, app version, the error), and whether each app session ended normally or in a crash
  • Your subscription status, as reported by the App Store through our purchase provider
  • If you installed the app from an Apple Search Ads advertisement: the attribution token Apple's AdServices framework gives the app. Our purchase provider, RevenueCat, exchanges it with Apple for the ad campaign that led to the install (for example the campaign, ad group and keyword, and the country) and stores that with your purchase history

We do not show ads in the app, we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell personal information. The Search Ads attribution above only tells us which of our own App Store ads led to an install or a purchase; it contains no identifier for following you across apps, and it is not used for that.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the app: store your pets' records and photos, sync them across your devices, and share them with the caregivers you invite
  • To send the reminders and notifications you turn on
  • To provide Premium features you have purchased
  • To diagnose crashes and keep the app reliable
  • To learn which of our App Store ads lead to installs and purchases
  • To respond to you and to handle account deletion and data export requests

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performing our contract with you to provide the app, on your consent for notifications, and on our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and working and in knowing which of our ads work.

4. Who processes data for us

We use a small number of service providers, each bound to process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud) — database, file storage and server functions, hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Signing in (Firebase Authentication) is a global Google service, so the details used to sign in may be processed outside the EU, under Google's data processing terms and the EU standard contractual clauses
  • Google (Gmail) — sends the sign-in and password reset codes you ask for by email
  • RevenueCat — manages in-app purchases and subscription status, and records which Apple Search Ads campaign, if any, led to the install
  • Apple — App Store payments, Sign in with Apple, push notification delivery, and Search Ads attribution
  • Expo — relays push notifications to Apple, and delivers app updates (EAS Update): when the app checks for an update, Expo receives the app version, the platform and the network address of the request, and no pet, photo or account data
  • Sentry — crash reporting, app session health, performance measurements (how long screens and requests take) and diagnostics. You can turn crash reports off in the app

RevenueCat, Sentry and Expo are based in the United States, so what they receive may be processed there. For those transfers we rely on the data processing terms each of them offers, which include the EU standard contractual clauses.

Payments are made through your Apple account; we never receive your payment card details.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users or others.

5. Sharing care of a pet

When you invite someone to care for a pet, they can see that pet's profile, care logs and health records, and — if you make them an editor — add to them. They cannot see your other pets or your photo album. You can change their role or remove them at any time. Their access to the pet's records ends immediately, and a document they already had open stops opening within five minutes, because documents are only ever opened through links that expire that quickly.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. If you delete your account in the app, we delete — immediately — your account, your pets' records, your photos and your documents, and the customer record our purchase provider holds for you. Where you cared for someone else's pet, what you added stays in their records because it is their pet's history, but your name and email address are removed from it, and invitations addressed to you are deleted. Copies in our providers' encrypted backups go as those backups expire, which for Google takes up to about six months. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

If you use the app without creating an account, your data is tied to an anonymous account on this device; signing out of it or uninstalling the app makes that data unreachable. Because nobody can sign back in to such an account, we delete it, and everything in it, after 30 days without use — unless it holds a purchase. Create an account to keep your pets' records safe.

7. Your rights and choices

You can:

  • See and correct your data in the app
  • Export all of your data from Profile → Export My Data. The file holds every record we store about your account, including who you share pets with; your photos and documents are listed in it by name, and you can save the ones you want from the app
  • Delete your account and all of its data from Profile → Delete Account
  • Turn notifications off in the app or in iOS Settings
  • Turn crash reports off from Profile → Data & Privacy → Crash Reports; the app then stops sending them

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. To exercise any right, contact us at cankanbur1@gmail.com.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to records is enforced on our servers for every request, so a pet's records can only be read by its owner and the caregivers they invited. Medical documents are stored privately under the same rule and are opened only through links that expire within five minutes.

Photos — your moments, your pets' photos and your profile photo — are stored at long, unguessable web addresses. The app shows a photo's address only to you, and a pet's photo to that pet's caregivers, but anyone who is given that address can open the photo until it is deleted.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify you and the relevant authorities of a breach as the law requires.

9. Children's privacy

Pet Moments is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version in the app and at https://canknbr.github.io/pet-moments-legal/privacy.html, and change the effective date. Material changes will be highlighted in the app.
