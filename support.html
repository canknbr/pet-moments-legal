Subscriptions

Premium is billed by Apple. To change or cancel a subscription, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.

Restoring a purchase

In the app, open the Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple account you bought with.

Your data

Export everything from Profile → Export My Data. Delete your account and all of its data from Profile → Delete Account.
